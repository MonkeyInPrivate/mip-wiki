# Thank you for all of your support, goodbye MIP Wiki!

I can't thank you all for your support before and during the development of this project, it's definitely been a journey. So much has changed in the past few months and many of the tools the community used have changed or even been deprecated, and I unfortunately just haven't had the time to maintain these guides. The Kindle Modding Wiki now is a significant resource that should help most people get started, and projects to help with the rest are in the works. I myself am working on porting this wiki to the Kindle Modding Wiki, which I'm hoping to release before the end of the year. 

That being said, if you still need the guides, they are still up on this site, but please make sure to confirm any information before following them, **nobody** except yourself is responsible for any damage or issues that doing so may result in.
